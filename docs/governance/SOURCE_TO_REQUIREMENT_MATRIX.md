## 2. Interpretation rules

- `PENDING SOURCE`, `RECORDED`, and `REGISTERED` are preserved exactly from the register.
- Source status, registry status, charter status, owner, phase, and dependencies are separate fields.
- `TBD` means the source did not specify the field; it is not inferred.
- `SOURCE VERIFICATION PENDING` is not `RESOLVED`.
- Administrative closure of D04-Task-07 means unresolved items were retained in the register; it does not mean the TBDs were solved.
- No per-message calendar date is inferred; dates are `NOT PRESENT IN EXPORT`.
